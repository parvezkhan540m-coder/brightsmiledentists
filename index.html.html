Meet the Doctor

Dr. Sarah Mitchell, DDS

Dr. Sarah Mitchell is a board-certified dentist with over 15 years of experience transforming smiles across Austin. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Texas School of Dentistry and completed advanced training in cosmetic and restorative dentistry at NYU College of Dentistry.

Dr. Mitchell founded Bright Smile Dental in 2009 with a simple mission: to make exceptional dental care accessible, comfortable, and even enjoyable. She is a proud member of the American Dental Association, the Texas Dental Association, and the Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

When she's not in the clinic, Dr. Mitchell volunteers at Austin Community Health Fairs and mentors dental students at UT Austin. She's a passionate advocate for preventive care and loves empowering her patients with the knowledge to maintain healthy smiles at home.
